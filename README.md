This game I made is about the player being chased by an enemy (the witch). 
You have three hearts, currently represented by the dots at the top left of the screen, and each time the red square touches you, you lose one.
The green squares are trees serving as obstacles, and each time you load the game, the pattern of trees is randomised.
Once the enemy attacks you, it cannot harm you until you get out of range.
If you lead the enemy into a corner, it can teleport past that corner.
